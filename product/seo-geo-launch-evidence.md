# Focused offer and launch evidence

Updated 16 September 2026. This is the actual evidence register for the focused product, not a statement that launch or market validation is complete.

## Current content offer

Version 4 is **Starter US$29** (12 articles per billing month, monthly AI checks of 3 questions across 4 services) and **Growth US$89** (30 articles, weekly checks of 10 questions across 4 services, up to 4 first-accepted existing-page improvements). Both include one website, one language, two seats, the calendar, automatic publishing with optional review, and search/connected purchase results. See [two-plan implementation](two-plan-pricing.md) for counting, plan transitions and activation.

Historical versions 1–3 remain pinned to existing subscriptions. Version 3 Content Growth stays at its original US$89/30-article arrangement. No customer subscription has been migrated by this work. The local deployment has no Stripe key or new price IDs configured; real checkout and commercial validation remain outstanding. Software tests and simulations do not establish willingness to pay, delivery cost or acquisition results.

## Historical version 2 offer

Local Search, plan version 2: **US$89 per website per month**. One language, up to 20 selected existing pages, two seats and one website connection. Four first-accepted page-improvement proposals per billing period. A proposal contains no more than five bounded supported edits. Acceptance uses a unit even if the owner later elects not to publish it; publication always requires its own action. Rejected drafts and no-change results consume none. Further revisions, technical retries and recovery for the same proposal never consume another improvement unit, including across a billing-period boundary.

The three-day trial requires a payment method at checkout and includes one first-accepted improvement. Checkout presents its actual trial end and recurring amount. It demonstrates the workflow; it cannot demonstrate search or purchase results in three days. Provider price validation refuses a mismatched amount, currency or monthly interval. No new plan has been assigned to existing customers implicitly, and no real checkout or customer charge was made during implementation.

New-article production is excluded from this version 2 allowance. Existing version 1 article entitlements and history remain pinned to their original arrangement. Social workflows are retired. Website support is the documented WordPress core page/post subset and the verified custom receiver contract; unsupported layouts require a precise assisted handoff. This offer does not include arbitrary page-builder editing, backlinks, Maps/review management, a CRM or agency tooling.

Four improvements is the initial package to test, not a measured sustainable promise. The prior plan called for deriving the allowance from delivery cost. The implementation now makes that counting rule concrete and reviewable; paid external rollout still requires evidence that this scope and price are workable.

## How to record the next external decision

For each of up to five comparable local businesses, record the business and decision maker, service/market, website publishing suitability, current alternative, purchase-tracking readiness, a relevant sample article and proposed content calendar, exact offer shown, their stated reason for accepting or declining, actual commitment/payment evidence, onboarding date, weekly owner-review and operator minutes, subsequent verified publications, and renewal decision. Retain declined cases in the denominator. A conversation, trial signup or an internal test is not a paid commitment. No contact is selected or messaged without owner authorization.

The initial demand gate remains at least three paid commitments from five businesses approached. Each commitment must follow a concrete relevant proposal and the documented offer. Record both approached and proposal-presented counts so failures to reach a decision do not disappear from the approached denominator. Four weekly workflow cycles and renewal evidence follow separately; the longer outcome assessment uses actual elapsed dates and a consistent purchase definition. Low-volume results can remain inconclusive.

## Cost interpretation

`/delivery-economics` provides calendar-month provider cost, actual recorded operator time, unpriced work, first-acceptance units and owner review actions. Owner timer intervals are not added to operator labour costs. Corrections append a replacement entry; the original remains in history and is excluded from the current sum. Missing records do not establish zero work.

The US$89 comparison is a package scenario, not collected revenue or profit. Unknown or ambiguous provider charges and unpriced human work prevent a complete scenario result. Even a known subtotal excludes unrecorded work, hosting, payment fees, tax and other overhead. USD provider costs must not be subtracted from a legacy EUR subscription price without a documented currency conversion. No sustainable-margin claim is supported yet.
